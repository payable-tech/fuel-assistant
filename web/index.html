          What the numbers mean
          Diesel bought
          Gallons of diesel bought with fuel cards, as Datatruck records them.
          Fuel spend
          What EFS billed the company for those gallons, after discounts.
          Price per gallon
          Fuel spend divided by gallons. "Pump price" is what the same gallons cost at the posted price.
          Discount kept
          Pump price minus what the company paid. Mostly the Love's discount; other chains give a few cents.
          Kept from drivers
          Discount on fuel billed to drivers. They are charged the pump price (most cards add a fee), so the discount stays with the company.
          Market price
          The U.S. Energy Information Administration's (EIA) average retail diesel price, published every Monday for each region of the country. Each gallon is priced at its region's price, between the Mondays before and after the purchase. Where our drivers fuel can't move it, so it is the fair yardstick for what we pay.
          Saved per gallon
          Market price minus what we paid, per gallon. Market ups and downs move both sides, so changes in this number come from our fuel decisions: where, when and how much drivers buy. Higher is better.
          Discount missed
          For fuel bought outside Love's: the gallons times how much smaller their discount was than this week's Love's average.
          Bought at Love's
          Share of diesel gallons bought at a station whose name starts with "LOVES".
          This week so far
          Monday up to the newest purchase, compared with last week up to the same weekday and time.
          Week
          Monday to Sunday, the company's week in Datatruck. Every time on the dashboard is US Central time, whatever this computer's time zone.
