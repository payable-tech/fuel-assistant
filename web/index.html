                What the numbers mean
                Diesel bought
                Gallons of diesel bought with fuel cards, as Datatruck records them.
                Fuel spend
                What EFS billed the company for those gallons, after discounts.
                Price per gallon
                Fuel spend divided by gallons. "Pump price" is what the same gallons cost at the posted price.
                Discount kept
                Pump price minus what the company paid. Mostly the Love's discount; other chains give a few cents.
                Kept from drivers
                Discount on fuel billed to drivers. They are charged the pump price (most cards add a fee), so the discount stays with the company.
                Middle 80% of gallons
                On the Love's chart, the shaded range: 1 gallon in 10 was bought below it and 1 in 10 above it, counted in gallons so a big fill weighs more than a small one. The single lowest and highest fills are in the tooltip; they are one odd station (California, a fill with no discount) and jump from week to week.
                Love's network average
                The Love's discount averaged over all its ~600 stores, from Love's daily price sheets, not just the stores we bought at. Shown from Oct 14, 2026 (the sheets before it have a gap).
                Paid extra outside Love's
                What the gallons bought outside Love's cost above the same gallons at that week's Love's price per gallon. Below zero: outside was cheaper that week.
                Paid above the cheapest Love's nearby
                Live (outside Love's): for each fill, what we paid minus the lowest price at any Love's within 25 miles (straight line) that day, from Love's price sheets. A Love's fill is at its store; any other at the truck's Samsara GPS then. The road ahead isn't known, so a Love's behind the truck counts too: it is the most that could have been saved. Below zero: the fill was cheaper than any Love's nearby.
                Bought at Love's
                Share of diesel gallons bought at a station whose name starts with "LOVES".
                This week so far
                Monday up to the newest purchase, compared with last week up to the same weekday and time.
                Week
                Monday to Sunday, the company's week in Datatruck. Every time on the dashboard is US Central time, whatever this computer's time zone.

                Love's: promised vs charged

                Every diesel fill at Love's: what EFS charged per gallon against that store's net price on that day's Love's price sheet. Within a cent counts as promised. "No deal": the store isn't on the sheet and gave no discount; its dollars are the discount missed at that day's average Love's discount (estimated). "No sheet that day": no Love's sheet was saved for that day, so it can't be checked.

                Fills not as promised

                Bought vs burned, per truck

                Diesel bought on this statement against the fuel each truck's engine used over the same days (Samsara). Into the tank: the gauge's change from the first day to the last, times the truck's tank (gallons ÷ gauge rise of its own fills). Unexplained = bought − burned − into the tank: diesel the truck doesn't account for. Biggest first.

                Each fill's evidence

                Every diesel fill against Samsara: the gauge's biggest rise from 4 h before Datatruck's time to 1 h after (that time can be hours late), and where the truck was when the gauge topped out. At the station: within 5 miles of the Love's store (the price sheet's store points can be a mile or two off); at other stations, in the statement's state. Rose less than the gallons: they would need a tank over 300 gal for that rise (tanks here work out at 127-199 gal). Didn't rise: 20 gal or more bought, the gauge moved under 3 points.
